7 Sequential Stages of Extraction

01

Seed Selection

We carefully select quality seeds suitable for oil extraction before beginning the manufacturing process.

02

Cleaning & Grading

The selected seeds are cleaned and graded to remove dust, debris and unwanted materials before pressing.

03

Sun Preparation

Seeds are naturally prepared and dried to maintain suitable moisture conditions for gentle extraction.

04

Slow Wood Pressing

The prepared seeds are slowly pressed using a traditional wooden extraction process.

05

Natural Cloth Filtering

Freshly extracted oil is naturally filtered through clean cloth to remove remaining solid particles.

06

Purity & Taste Check

Each batch is checked for appearance, aroma and overall quality before it moves to packaging.

07

Clean Glass Packaging

The finished oil is carefully packed in suitable food-grade containers for safe storage and delivery.
