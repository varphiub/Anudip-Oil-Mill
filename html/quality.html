Gate 01 grain

Raw Seed Moisture & Aflatoxin Screening

Infrared moisture analyzer ensures seeds have ≤ 6% moisture. Rigorous UV fluorescence screening for zero mold or aflatoxins before entering storage.

  • check Zero Aflatoxins / Mold
  • check Manual Kernel Grading
Gate 02 thermostat

Digital Extraction Temp Logging

Calibrated thermocouples monitor the oil output stream continuously during crushing to guarantee the temperature strictly remains below 45°C.

  • check Real-time <45°C Logging
  • check Vaagai Wood Heat Dissipation
  • check Zero Friction Burn
Gate 03 filter_alt

Natural Gravity & Cloth Straining

Oil undergoes 48-hour static sedimentation allowing coarse seed micro-fibers to settle naturally, followed by gravity straining through virgin unbleached cotton.

  • check 48-Hour Natural Settling

  • check Zero Chemical Filter Clays

  • check 100% Negative Argemone Test

Consumer Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions on Quality

Everything you need to know about authentic cold-pressed purity, storage, and natural characteristics.

Fine sediment is the hallmark of genuine, unrefined cold-pressed oil! Because we use traditional gravity sedimentation and cotton cloth filtering rather than chemical clays or high-pressure micro-sieves, beneficial micro-seed nutrients settle naturally. Simply shake lightly or use directly.

1. Pure Aroma Test: Cold-pressed oil possesses the rich, authentic natural aroma of the raw seed, whereas refined oil has zero smell.
2. Cold Test (Coconut Oil): Pure virgin coconut oil completely solidifies into pure white crystal butter below 24°C.
3. Non-Sticky Feel: Pure cold-pressed oil absorbs smoothly into the skin without greasy synthetic mineral residue.

Unlike commercial refined oils that add synthetic silicone anti-foaming agents (like Dimethylpolysiloxane / DMPS), pure cold-pressed oil contains natural phytosterols and moisture that release delicate bubbles upon first heating. This is 100% natural and proof of zero anti-foaming chemicals.

Store your oil in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Because our oils are extracted without heat and contain natural Vitamin E antioxidants, they remain fresh and aromatic for 6 to 9 months without any synthetic preservatives.
